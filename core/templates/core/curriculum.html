A broad, balanced, and globally competitive academic framework.

ROYAL BEES SCHOOLS offers a comprehensive curriculum based on the Nigerian National Policy on Education, enriched with international best practices to provide a globally competitive education. Our curriculum is designed to be broad, balanced, and highly relevant to the evolving needs of our students.

Educational Progression

Tailored learning stages designed for optimal student development.

Creche & Pre-Nursery

A safe, loving environment focused on early childhood development, sensory exploration, and interactive play-based learning.

Nursery (KG Classes)

Laying the foundation for academic success with an engaging focus on Numeracy, Literacy, Phonics, Creative Arts, and positive Social Habits.

Primary (Basic 1 - 6)

A robust foundational stage covering core subjects including English Studies, Mathematics, Basic Science & Technology (BST), National Values Education (NVE), Pre-Vocational Studies (PVS), Cultural & Creative Arts (CCA), French, and Nigerian Languages.

Junior Secondary (JSS 1-3 / Basic 7-9)

Building upon primary education by introducing a wider, more specialized range of subjects, thoroughly preparing students for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

Senior Secondary (SSS 1-3)

Advanced specialization in Science, Arts, or Commercial pathways. Rigorous preparation for WASSCE, NECO SSCE, and vital international examinations to ensure university readiness.

Core Subject Areas

While specific subjects vary by level and stream, our core academic pillars include:

English & Literature

English Language, Literature-in-English, Phonics, and Diction.

Mathematics

Core Mathematics, Further Mathematics, and Quantitative Reasoning.

The Sciences

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Basic Science, and Agricultural Science.

Languages

French, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, and advanced communication skills.

Humanities

Government, History, Geography, Economics, and Civic Education.

Technology

Basic Technology, ICT/Computer Studies, and Technical Drawing.

Vocational & Arts

Business Studies, Accounting, Home Economics, and Visual Arts.
